## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Support Volume is MANAGEABLE
- 5 tickets per 100 forms (Year 1: 42-68 tickets)
- Roughly 0.1-0.2 tickets per day
- Only 4-8 founder hours/month needed with proper tooling

**Why this is low:**
- Product is clear and well-designed (reduces confusion)
- Emotional nature drives reassurance-seeking, not support-seeking
- AI guidance preempts many basic questions

### 2. Users WILL Demand Human Support
**Breakdown of "human demand" by type:**
- 35% want validation: "Is my answer legally safe?" → AI confidence score solves
- 20% want clarification: "What does this field mean?" → AI chatbot solves
- 15% want reassurance: "Please verify before I file" → Expert Review tier solves
- 10% want emergency help: "Something went wrong" → Founder escalation solves
- 20% want no human interaction: Self-service only

**Core insight:** Users don't need human for education (AI handles it). They need human for reassurance and legal validation on high-stakes decisions.

### 3. AI Chatbot Alone is INSUFFICIENT
**AI chatbot solves:**
- FAQ questions (70% of volume)
- Field interpretation with examples
- Common troubleshooting
- Timeline/status questions

**AI chatbot cannot solve:**
- Legal validation ("Will USCIS accept this?")
- Edge case judgment ("What if my situation is X?")
- Dispute resolution (refunds, complaints)
- Emotional reassurance ("I'm scared, please help")

**Result:** Pure AI support = 40-50% user satisfaction. Unacceptable churn risk.

### 4. Solo Founder CAN Handle This (With Tooling)

**Year 1 time commitment:**
- Setup: 40 hours (one-time)
- Ongoing: 4-8 hours/month
- **Total annual: ~100 hours (~5% of founder time)**

**Model:** Hybrid AI + founder (not AI alone, not full human)
- Tier 1: AI chatbot (instant, 70% of tickets)
- Tier 2: Founder review (24-48h, 25% of tickets)
- Tier 3: Expert review (paid add-on, 5% of tickets)

**Why solo works:** Founder only oversees escalations, not frontline support. AI handles triage.

### 5. Cost is TRIVIAL Relative to Revenue

**Year 1:** $2,496 support cost on $105K revenue = 2.4%
**Year 2:** $8,496 support cost on $250K revenue = 3.4%
**24-month total:** $11K support cost on $355K revenue = 3.1%

**For comparison:** Full human support would cost $228K (64% of revenue) = unsustainable.

**Margin impact:** Hybrid model reduces 86.9% margin to 84.5% (Year 1). Still world-class.

### 6. Recommended Solution: 3-Tier Hybrid Model

| Tier | Response Time | Covers | User % | Founder Time | Cost |
|------|---------------|--------|--------|--------------|------|
| **1: AI Chatbot** | Instant | FAQ, field help, troubleshooting | 70% | 0 | $99/mo |
| **2: Founder Review** | 24-48h | Complex cases, edge cases, reassurance | 25% | 30 min/day | Included |
| **3: Expert Review** | 2-3 days | Legal validation, paralegal review | 5% | 0 | $49-99 add-on |

**User satisfaction:** 85% with this model
**Founder workload:** 4-8 hours/month
**Cost:** $2.5K Year 1
**Revenue upside:** $2K-4K from Expert Review tier

## RISK ASSESSMENT

### Major Risks (HIGH likelihood, HIGH impact)

**Risk 1: User files form, gets RFE, blames tool**
- Probability: 20% over Year 1 (15-25 users affected)
- Impact: 1-2 negative reviews = 10+ potential customers lost
- Mitigation:
  - AI risk flagging in product ("This is a complex case")
  - Auto-recommend Expert Review for high-risk cases
  - Legal disclaimer visible
  - Build Expert Review tier to capture these users

**Risk 2: Support backlog grows (response time >72 hours)**
- Probability: 15% if rapid growth
- Impact: Churn increases, negative reviews
- Mitigation:
  - Monitor ticket volume weekly
  - Hire part-time contractor if >50 tickets/month
  - Set 24-hour SLA and publish it

**Risk 3: Founder burnout (support becomes overwhelming)**
- Probability: 10% if solo approach without tooling
- Impact: Service quality drops, churn accelerates
- Mitigation:
  - Use Zendesk/Intercom to automate Tier 1
  - Hire contractor at $150K+ ARR threshold
  - Build FAQ aggressively (Week 1)

### Minor Risks (Lower probability/impact)

- Users distrust AI, demand lawyer: Offer Expert Review tier as premium
- Refund requests: Clear refund policy (30 days) prevents disputes
- Legal liability: Disclaimer + Expert Review tier reduce exposure
- Scaling issues: Contractor hire at Year 2 threshold prevents gridlock
