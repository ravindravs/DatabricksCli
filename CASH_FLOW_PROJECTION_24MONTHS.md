- Month 1: 50 visitors, 0.5 conversions, grow 1% MoM
- Rationale: Slow SEO ramp, low initial awareness, limited word-of-mouth

- Month 1: 100 visitors, 1 conversion, grow 2% MoM
- Rationale: Moderate SEO growth, word-of-mouth from early users, Product Hunt launch effect

- Month 4+: Stabilized growth as SEO establishes

- Month 1: 150 visitors, 1.5 conversions, grow 4% MoM
- Rationale: Viral word-of-mouth in diaspora communities, strong Product Hunt launch, immediate SEO traction
