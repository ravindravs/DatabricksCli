## The 6 Drop-Off Risks (Ordered by Impact)

| # | Stage | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|-------|------|--------|-----------|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Critical** | **3. Trial→Paywall** | Trial too short; user can't decide | Lose 50-70% of trial users | Extend trial to 5-7 Q's (≥2 min of value) |
| ⭐⭐ **High** | **2. Landing Page** | Weak value prop; no social proof | Lose 50-70% of visitors | Add testimonials, fast load, clear $29 price |
| ⭐⭐ **High** | **5. Form Completion** | Mid-form confusion; no progress bar | Lose 20-40% at form Q's | Progress indicator, save-for-later, examples |
| ⭐ **Medium** | **1. Awareness** | Poor SEO ranking (#6-10 instead of #1) | Lose 40-60% of organic traffic | Publish 20+ blog posts; build internal links |
| ⭐ **Medium** | **6. Post-Download** | User doesn't know next steps | Lose 15% of people who don't mail | Email checklist, phone support, QR codes |
| ⭐ **Medium** | **4. Payment** | Payment friction (slow, no options) | Lose 10-30% at checkout | Stripe + PayPal, <1 sec load, SSL badge |

## Key Success Factors

### MUST-HAVE (Non-negotiable)
- [ ] USCIS-compatible PDF (correct coordinates, checkboxes, fonts)
- [ ] Plain-language Q&A (6th-grade reading level)
- [ ] Consistency checker (flags 40+ RFE risks)
- [ ] Stripe integration (secure, fast checkout)
- [ ] Email confirmation + PDF attachment (within 30 sec)
- [ ] Checklist of required documents (printable)
- [ ] Clear "how to mail" instructions (state-specific USCIS address)

### CRITICAL FOR CONVERSION (>10% impact each)
- [ ] Social proof (5+ testimonials, 4.5+ star rating)
- [ ] Landing page <1.5 sec load time (mobile-first)
- [ ] Trial length: 5-7 questions (minimum viable value)
- [ ] Progress bar in form (Q 23/60, ~12 min left)
- [ ] Real-time error flagging (yellow/red warnings)

### NICE-TO-HAVE (Post-launch)
- [ ] Multi-language support (Spanish priority)
- [ ] Case tracking integration (USCIS status)
- [ ] "Save for later" (resumable session)
- [ ] Referral link generation ($5 credit per ref)
- [ ] 3-form bundle pricing ($69 vs. $87)
