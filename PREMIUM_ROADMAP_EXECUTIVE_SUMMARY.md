## TOP 3 FEATURES THAT JUSTIFY PRICE INCREASES

### Feature #1: Case Status Tracking ($15-20 premium)
**Solves:** "Where is my case? When will I hear back?"

- **Customer pain:** Manually checking USCIS website weekly (50+ hours over 2 years)
- **Value delivered:** Email status updates + timeline visualization + anxiety reduction
- **Replaces:** $200-300 in lawyer status inquiry fees
- **Build effort:** 4 weeks
- **Revenue potential:** $1,500+/month (20% of Premium tier customers)
- **Why it works:** Every filer wants to know their case status; creates stickiness

### Feature #2: Expert Form Review ($20-25 premium)
**Solves:** "Did I fill this out correctly? Will USCIS reject it?"

- **Customer pain:** Fear of RFE (Request for Evidence) due to incomplete/inconsistent form
- **Value delivered:** Paralegals review form + flag 40+ common errors + provide written feedback
- **Replaces:** $500-1,000 lawyer form review fee
- **Build effort:** 8 weeks (includes hiring paralegals)
- **Revenue potential:** $2,000+/month (included in Premium)
- **Why it works:** Reduces RFE risk by 70%; customers save thousands

### Feature #3: RFE Response Helper ($25-30 premium)
**Solves:** "I got an RFE. How do I respond to avoid denial?"

- **Customer pain:** RFE determines case outcome; hiring lawyer for response = $500-1,000
- **Value delivered:** AI-guided RFE response builder + evidence checklist + quality review
- **Replaces:** $500-1,000 lawyer RFE consultation fee
- **Build effort:** 6 weeks
- **Revenue potential:** $1,250+/month (included in Elite tier)
- **Why it works:** Only triggers when RFE received (high intent); highest value feature

## RISK ANALYSIS

### Top 3 Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Support costs exceed revenue** | Cap paralegals to 5 cases/day; use AI chatbot for tier 1; limit support hours |
| **Tier cannibalization** | Keep Standard simple + cheap ($29); only target Premium to power users |
| **Legal liability** | Strong disclaimers, professional liability insurance, legal review of all content |

### Implementation Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature takes 2x longer to build | Medium | High | Pre-plan architecture; use MVPs (minimum viable features) |
| Paralegals give bad advice | Low | Critical | Training + QA process; strong disclaimers; professional insurance |
| USCIS API breaks | Low | Medium | Fall back to manual status entry; maintain backup system |
| Low Premium adoption | Medium | Medium | Pre-announce features; collect customer feedback; A/B test messaging |
