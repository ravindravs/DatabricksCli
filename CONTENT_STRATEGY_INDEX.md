## CORE STRATEGIC PRINCIPLES

### 1. "Help First, Sell Second"
- Content solves problems without pushing the tool
- Articles are useful even if reader never sees the product
- Trust builds naturally through helpfulness
- Tool becomes obvious solution at the end

### 2. "Write What Users Actually Search For"
- Target real keywords (80K searches for "how to fill I-130")
- Answer questions people ask on Reddit/forums
- Focus on intent (solve their problem)

### 3. "Build Topical Authority"
- Create 70+ articles on one vertical (immigration forms)
- All articles support each other (internal linking)
- Google rewards topical authority with higher rankings

### 4. "Leverage Diaspora Word-of-Mouth"
- Immigration communities share tools virally
- WhatsApp, WeChat, Facebook groups drive organic distribution
- Content validates tool as legitimate (not scammy)
- One approval leads to 5-10 referrals

### 5. "Zero CAC Model"
- No paid advertising (saves $30K+/year)
- Organic search captures all demand
- Word-of-mouth multiplies effect
- Community engagement feels natural (not marketing)
