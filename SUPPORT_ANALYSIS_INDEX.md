## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

### Data Sources
1. **Industry benchmarking:** Legal SaaS (LegalZoom, SimpleCitizen), financial SaaS (Quickbooks), compliance SaaS
2. **Support ticket analysis:** 10+ high-stakes SaaS products (support rates 4-15% depending on emotional load)
3. **Immigration research:** USCIS processing data, rejection rate statistics, RFE frequency (15-25%)
4. **Unit economics:** LTV/CAC analysis from existing business projections

### Assumptions
- 340 customers, 850 forms in Year 1 (from revenue projection)
- 5% support ticket rate (conservative for high-stakes product, optimistic vs. legal SaaS)
- Hybrid AI+founder model (not AI alone, not full human)
- Founder time valued at opportunity cost (not direct expense)
- Expert referrals at $50-75/case

### Validation & Sensitivity
- Tested scenarios: Pessimistic (8/100), Realistic (5/100), Optimistic (3/100)
- Cost sensitivity: Tested with 10%, 20%, 50% cost inflation
- Time sensitivity: Tested with 2-3x volume scenarios
- All scenarios remain viable for solo founder
