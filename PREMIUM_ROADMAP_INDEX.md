## TOP 3 FEATURES THAT JUSTIFY PRICE INCREASES

### 1. Case Status Tracking (+$15-20 Premium)
**What:** Real-time USCIS case status updates via email
**Why:** Users spend 50+ hours manually checking case status over 2-year processing period
**Value:** $200-300 in lawyer status inquiry fees saved
**Build effort:** 4 weeks
**Revenue potential:** $1,500+/month

### 2. Expert Form Review (+$20-25 included in Premium)
**What:** Paralegals review filled form + flag 40+ common errors
**Why:** Prevents RFE (Request for Evidence) which costs $2,000+ in lawyer fees
**Value:** $500-1,000 lawyer review fee saved
**Build effort:** 8 weeks (includes hiring)
**Revenue potential:** $2,000+/month

### 3. RFE Response Helper (+$25-30 included in Elite)
**What:** AI-guided RFE response builder with evidence checklist
**Why:** RFE response determines case outcome; hiring lawyer = $500-1,000
**Value:** $500-1,000 lawyer consultation saved
**Build effort:** 6 weeks
**Revenue potential:** $1,250+/month

## GO/NO-GO DECISION CHECKLIST

### Before Launching Premium Tier ($59):
- [ ] 100+ Standard customers with NPS >40
- [ ] Case tracking feature built and tested (4 weeks)
- [ ] Support load manageable (<10 hours/week)
- [ ] Paralegals hired or contractors identified
- [ ] Pricing page A/B tested

### Before Launching Elite Tier ($99):
- [ ] Premium tier has 15%+ adoption (15+ customers)
- [ ] RFE helper feature built and tested (6 weeks)
- [ ] Lawyer network has 10+ signed partners
- [ ] Support staffed (1 FTE minimum)
- [ ] LTV/CAC ratio healthy (>50x)

### Red Flags (Don't Launch):
- ❌ Feature takes >8 weeks to build (unless it's defining Elite feature)
- ❌ <100 customers requesting it
- ❌ Requires full-time staff (unless commission model)
- ❌ Creates legal liability without mitigation
- ❌ Is "nice to have" not "must have"

## FAQ

**Q: Why 3 tiers instead of 2?**
A: Research shows 3-tier pricing maximizes revenue (premium anchors middle tier, elite captures high-willingness-to-pay segment). 2-tier pricing leaves 10-15% of revenue on table.

**Q: Isn't $99 too expensive?**
A: $99 Elite replaces $2,000+ in lawyer fees. Customers will pay for risk reduction. Target 10-15% of customers (high-stakes cases), not 50%.

**Q: Will Standard tier customers feel like they're missing out?**
A: No, if Standard tier is still excellent ($29 for form + checklist + basic support). Premium/Elite are for customers with complex cases or repeat purchases.

**Q: What if adoption is lower than projected?**
A: Contingency: Drop Premium to $49, expand features, run A/B testing. Conservative projection assumes 60-75% adoption; even at 50% adoption you get +50% revenue lift.

**Q: Do we have the team to support this?**
A: Phase 1 needs 1.7 FTE engineers + 2-3 paralegals (contractors). Phase 2 needs 4 FTE total. Budget for hiring.

**Q: When should we launch if V1 isn't shipped yet?**
A: Wait until V1 has 50-100 Standard customers + positive feedback. Then launch Premium tier 4 weeks later.
