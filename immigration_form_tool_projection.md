## KEY INSIGHTS

1. **High Margin Business**: With 86.1% net profit margin, this is a highly profitable model with minimal operational overhead.

2. **Organic Growth Trajectory**: 2% monthly growth compounds to 22% year-over-year growth, creating strong unit economics without marketing spend.

3. **Cost Structure Efficiency**:
   - Hosting represents the largest cost (9.2% of revenue) due to fixed infrastructure
   - API costs are minimal (1.1%) with predictable per-form scaling
   - Payment processing fees (3.5%) are typical for SaaS

4. **Scalability**: As traffic grows, hosting costs increase but remain manageable. Consider serverless architecture to optimize fixed hosting costs.

5. **Monthly Profitability**: Every month is profitable from Month 1, with profit growing from $3,683 to $5,981 by Month 24.

6. **Break-even**: Not applicable - profitable from inception with $0 marketing spend.
