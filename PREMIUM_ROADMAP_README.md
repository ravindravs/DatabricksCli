## 🎯 THE STRATEGY IN 60 SECONDS

**Current state:** V1 at $29/form, Year 1 ARR = $103K
**Problem:** Customers need $500-2,100 worth of lawyer features we don't have
**Solution:** Build 3 tiers:
- **Standard ($29):** DIY filers, form completion
- **Premium ($59):** Complex cases, case tracking + expert review
- **Elite ($99):** High-stakes, RFE helper + lawyer referral + priority support

**Result:** Same 340 customers, ASP increases $29 → $55, Year 1 ARR = $207K-286K (+100-177%)

**Timeline:** 16 weeks, 3 phases
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4):** Case tracking → +$18K/year
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5-10):** RFE helper → +$42K/year
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 11-16):** Bundles & optimization → +$60K/year

**Investment:** 4-5 FTE + contractors
**ROI:** Payback in 3-6 months

## ✨ HIGHLIGHTS FROM THE RESEARCH

### What Makes This Strategy Work?

1. **Justified pricing:** Each tier replaces $200-2,100 in lawyer costs
2. **Proven market demand:** Customers literally pay lawyers $500+ for these features
3. **Same customer base:** No new acquisition needed; same 340 customers, higher price
4. **Phased approach:** Launch Premium first (low risk), Elite later (high payoff)
5. **Strong unit economics:** LTV/CAC improves from 120x to 200x+
6. **Defensible moat:** Case tracking + lawyer network create switching costs

### The Math Is Compelling

- **Standard stays $29:** No change for budget-conscious customers
- **Premium adds $30:** Justified by preventing $2,000 RFE cost
- **Elite adds $70:** Justified by replacing $2,100 in lawyer consulting
- **Result:** 100%+ revenue growth, same customer effort

### Timeline Is Realistic

- **Phase 1 (4 weeks):** Build case tracking, launch Premium tier
- **Phase 2 (6 weeks):** Build RFE helper, launch Elite tier, hire lawyers
- **Phase 3 (6 weeks):** Optimize, bundle, scale
- **Total:** 16 weeks = 4 months = Q2 (after Q1 V1 launch)
