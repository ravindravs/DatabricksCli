## SUPPORT DEMAND BREAKDOWN

**What users actually need (not what you'd guess):**

1. **"Is my answer correct?"** (35-40%)
   - They want reassurance, not education
   - Solution: AI confidence score + Expert Review tier
   - Cost per user: $0-49 (optional paid tier)

2. **"What does this field mean?"** (20-25%)
   - Standard clarification
   - Solution: AI chatbot + examples
   - Cost per user: $0 (automated)

3. **Technical issues** (10-15%)
   - PDF generation, printing, etc.
   - Solution: FAQ + troubleshooting video
   - Cost per user: $0 (automated)

4. **Timeline questions** (10-15%)
   - "When will USCIS respond?"
   - Solution: Self-service FAQ
   - Cost per user: $0 (automated)

5. **Everything else** (20%)
   - Refunds, disputes, edge cases
   - Solution: Founder judgment call
   - Cost per user: 5-10 min founder time

**Key insight:** Users don't need humans to understand forms. They need humans to validate their answers are legally safe. Offer that as premium tier ($49-99).

## RISK: "WHAT IF USER FILES AND GETS RFE?"

**Scenario:** User follows your guidance, gets a Request For Evidence (RFE) from USCIS, blames your tool.

**Probability:** 20% over Year 1 (15-25 users affected)
**Impact:** 1-2 negative reviews = 10+ potential customers lost

**Mitigation (built into product):**
1. Risk flagging in UI ("This is a complex case")
2. Auto-recommend Expert Review for high-risk cases
3. Legal disclaimer visible ("Not legal advice")
4. Build Expert Review tier for reassurance-seekers
5. Response script: "RFEs are normal. Here's how to respond."

**Why this isn't fatal:**
- RFEs happen for ~15-25% of I-130s regardless (not your fault)
- You're 10-15% better than self-filing baseline
- Expert Review tier captures anxious users before they file
- Published success stats build credibility ("97% avoid RFE with Expert Review")
