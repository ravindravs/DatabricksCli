## FEATURE JUSTIFICATION BY PRICE POINT

### Standard Tier: $29 (No Change)
**Customer mindset:** "I'll fill the form myself for cheap"

| Feature | Value Delivered | Time Saved | Justifies Price |
|---------|---|---|---|
| Interactive Q&A | $100 (vs. lawyer research) | 2 hours | YES |
| PDF auto-fill | $50 (vs. manual entry) | 1 hour | YES |
| Consistency checker | $300 (prevents RFE risk) | 2 hours | YES |
| Checklist + export | $50 (next steps clarity) | 1 hour | YES |
| **Total value:** | **$500+** | **6 hours** | **$29 is excellent deal** |

---

### Premium Tier: $59 (+$30 vs. Standard)
**Customer mindset:** "I need confidence this will work; worth extra $30 for peace of mind"

| Feature | Value Delivered | Time Saved | Justifies +$30 |
|---------|---|---|---|
| Case status tracking | $200 (vs. lawyer status calls) | 10 hours | YES |
| Expert form review | $500 (prevents $2K RFE) | 3 hours | YES |
| Interview prep | $200 (confidence + tips) | 4 hours | YES |
| Priority support (4-6hr) | $200 (peace of mind) | - | YES |
| Renewal reminders | $50 (prevents missed deadline) | 2 hours | YES |
| **Added value:** | **$1,150** | **19 hours** | **$30 is extreme discount** |

**Decision point:** If customer's case has complexity (spouse, children, background check) → Premium

---

### Elite Tier: $99 (+$70 vs. Standard, +$40 vs. Premium)
**Customer mindset:** "My case is complicated/high-stakes; I need expert help + lawyer access"

| Feature | Value Delivered | Time Saved | Justifies +$70 |
|---------|---|---|---|
| RFE response helper | $1,000 (replaces lawyer RFE response) | 8 hours | YES |
| Unlimited expert reviews | $300 (catch more issues) | 2 hours | YES |
| Lawyer consultation booking | $300 (direct lawyer access) | 3 hours | YES |
| 2hr support (vs. 4-6hr) | $200 (crisis management) | - | YES |
| Case strategy calls | $300 (expert guidance) | 4 hours | YES |
| **Added value vs. Standard:** | **$2,100** | **17 hours** | **$99 is incredible value** |

**Decision point:** If customer has high-stakes case (family sponsorship, background concerns, RFE received) → Elite

## QUICK DECISION: SHOULD YOU BUILD THIS?

**Answer "Yes" to all of these, you should launch premium tiers:**

- [ ] V1 has launched and has 50+ paying customers
- [ ] Customer NPS is >40 (customers love it)
- [ ] You have at least 1-2 requests for case tracking per week
- [ ] You can hire 2-3 paralegals (contractors) in next month
- [ ] You can dedicate 1 engineer for 4 weeks (Phase 1)
- [ ] You're not running out of cash in next 6 months
- [ ] You believe customers have budget for $59-99 pricing

**Answer "No" to any of these, wait 3-6 months:**

- [ ] V1 just launched (let it stabilize first)
- [ ] Customer NPS is <35 (fix V1 first)
- [ ] Support is overwhelming (scale operations first)
- [ ] You can't find good paralegals/contract help
- [ ] Cash runway is <6 months
- [ ] Customers consistently push back on any pricing increase
