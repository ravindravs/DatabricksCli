## PUBLISHING CADENCE & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

### Ideal Publishing Pace

**Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** 3-4 articles/week
- Target: 15 foundational articles
- Focus: Quality over quantity
- Buffer: 2 weeks for editing/revision

**Phase 2 (Months 4-9):** 2-3 articles/week
- Target: 30 articles for Scale phase
- Mix: 50% new + 50% case studies/FAQ
- Buffer: 1 week for revision

**Phase 3 (Months 10-18):** 2-3 articles/week
- Target: 25+ articles
- Mix: 40% new + 40% updates + 20% multimedia
- Buffer: Updates to top-performing content
