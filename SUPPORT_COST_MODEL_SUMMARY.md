## SUPPORT BURDEN BY PHASE

### Phase 1-3: Months 1-12 (0.3-0.5 tickets per day)

**What users need:**
1. **35%** - Answer validation ("Is this correct?") → AI confidence score
2. **20%** - Field clarification ("What does this mean?") → Chatbot
3. **15%** - PDF/technical issues → Troubleshooting guide
4. **10%** - Timeline questions ("When will USCIS respond?") → Self-service FAQ
5. **20%** - Misc (refunds, features, complaints) → Founder review

**Support approach:**
- Tier 1 (70%): AI chatbot (Zendesk/Intercom) - instant
- Tier 2 (25%): Founder review - 24-48 hours
- Tier 3 (5%): Expert referral - paid add-on

**Founder capacity:** 6-8 hours/month (5-7% of time)
**Cost:** $2,500/year (tools + expert referrals)

### Phase 4: Year 2 (0.4-0.5 tickets per day)

**Growth drivers:**
- Volume doubles (3,000+ forms)
- User expectations increase (want faster response)
- Complexity increases (multi-form cases, edge cases)

**Support approach:**
- Maintain Tier 1 (AI chatbot)
- Expand Tier 2: Add part-time contractor (8 hrs/week @ $25/hr)
- Expand Tier 3: More expert referrals for complex cases

**Founder capacity:** 8-10 hours/month (oversight + training)
**Cost:** $8,500/year (tools + contractor + expert referrals)

## HYBRID MODEL: HOW IT WORKS

### Architecture: 3-Tier Support

**Tier 1 - AI Chatbot (70% of volume, instant)**
- FAQ automation (Zendesk/Intercom)
- Field interpretation help
- Common troubleshooting
- Zero founder time
- User satisfaction: 80%
- Cost: $99/month

**Tier 2 - Founder Review (25% of volume, 24-48 hours)**
- Complex field clarification
- Edge case guidance
- Risk assessment
- Escalation decision
- Founder time: 30 min/day
- Cost: Founder time (no extra cash)

**Tier 3 - Expert Review (5% of volume, 2-3 business days)**
- Legal reassurance ("Is this safe?")
- Paralegal/lawyer review
- High-confidence validation
- Optional paid add-on ($49-99)
- Cost: $50-75 per review (referral)

**Net result:** Users get fast answers (Tier 1) + human review option (Tier 3) + founder oversight (Tier 2) without overwhelming founder.

## SUPPORT DEMAND ANALYSIS

### Will Users Demand Human Support?

**Finding: YES, but for reassurance, not education.**

User breakdown:
- **40%** - Just buy and use (no support needed)
- **35%** - Ask AI chatbot, satisfied with answers
- **20%** - Ask for human validation ("Can someone verify?")
- **5%** - Demand immediate human contact or refund

**How to handle demand:**
1. **Offer "Expert Review" tier** ($49-99) as optional add-on
2. **Auto-recommend** for high-risk cases (married <2 years, military cases, etc.)
3. **Show success rates** in UI ("99% of users who follow guidance avoid rejection")
4. **Provide legal disclaimer** (protect against liability)

**Expected uptake:**
- Expert Review: 30-40% of users add it ($2,000-4,000 Year 1 revenue)
- Escalations to founder: 5-10% of users request direct contact
- Refunds: 1-2% of users (manage with clear refund policy)

## AI CHATBOT vs. HUMAN SUPPORT

### Does AI Chatbot Work for Immigration Forms?

**What AI handles well (70% of volume):**
- "What does this field mean?" ✓
- "Can you give me an example?" ✓
- "What documents do I need?" ✓
- "How long does USCIS take?" ✓
- "Is my birthplace spelled correctly?" ✓

**What AI struggles with (30% of volume):**
- "Is my answer legally safe?" → AI can't guarantee
- "What if I have a criminal record?" → Edge case, needs human judgment
- "Will I get approved?" → Too much variance, needs expert
- "I want to talk to a real person" → Emotional support need
- Disputes (refunds, complaints) → Founder judgment call

**Recommendation:** AI as first filter, human as reassurance layer.

## RISK MANAGEMENT

### What Could Go Wrong?

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **User files form, gets RFE, blames tool** | 20% | HIGH | Risk flagging in UI + Expert Review tier |
| **Support backlog grows (response >72h)** | 15% | HIGH | Monitor ticket velocity, hire at threshold |
| **Users demand unlimited free support** | 25% | MEDIUM | Clear SLAs + refund policy |
| **Negative reviews due to support gaps** | 20% | MEDIUM | Fast response (24h SLA) + public replies |
| **Founder burnout from support load** | 10% | HIGH | Hire early if volume >20 hrs/month |

**Mitigation strategy:**
1. Set clear SLAs (24-hour first response, 48-hour resolution)
2. Build Expert Review tier for reassurance seekers
3. Document decision trees + FAQs aggressively
4. Monitor NPS and respond to complaints
5. Hire contractor at $150K+ ARR threshold
